Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salon Business Studio (“Salon”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when salon owners and their team members use our application, and when customers use booking pages or provide information to a salon through the application.

Important roles: The salon or business that collects appointment and customer information decides why it uses that information and is generally responsible for it. Salon provides the software and processes that information on the salon’s instructions. If you are a customer, contact the salon you booked with for questions about the booking information it holds.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password verifier (we do not store your password in plain text), account/session identifiers and account status.
  • Salon and team information: salon name, city, services, prices, working hours, staff profiles, roles and availability.
  • Customer and booking information: names, phone numbers, email addresses when provided, appointment details, service selections, booking status, customer notes and birthday information when entered by the salon.
  • Business records: payment and expense entries, payroll and commission information entered by the salon.
  • Consent records: marketing permission or opt-out details recorded by the salon, including channel, source, timestamp and any evidence note entered.
  • Connected calendar information: if a user connects Google Calendar, we receive the Google account email/profile information returned during authorization and OAuth tokens required for the connection. If a user connects Apple Calendar, we receive the Apple Account email and app-specific password supplied for that connection.
  • Technical information: session and authentication data, request metadata and information needed to secure, operate and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide salon management and appointment-booking features, display business records to authorized users, connect calendar accounts when requested, maintain service reliability, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use customer contact details entered by a salon to independently market our own products. The salon is responsible for having an appropriate legal basis and obtaining any required permission before sending promotional communications.

3. Calendar connections and Google user data

Connecting a calendar is optional and requires the account holder to authorize the relevant provider. For Google Calendar, the application requests the Google identity/email and calendar permission shown on Google's consent screen. The current connection flow stores the returned access and refresh tokens in encrypted form so the connection can be maintained. The connected account email is stored as the connection label.

Calendar connection is currently being developed. Connecting an account does not mean that appointment events are already being imported, exported or synchronized. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the user-requested calendar connection. We do not disclose Google user data except as needed to provide the feature, comply with law, or protect the service and its users.

You can disconnect a calendar from the application’s calendar settings. Disconnecting removes the connection from the application; to revoke the application's access completely, also remove Salon from your Google Account's third-party connections or manage access in the relevant calendar provider account.

4. Storage and service providers

Application account, salon, booking and business records are stored in a hosted database. The application and API are hosted using GitHub Pages and Cloudflare infrastructure. Calendar authorization is handled by Google or Apple when you choose to connect those services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Data may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Calendar credentials are encrypted before being stored by the application. Passwords are processed using a one-way password hashing method; session tokens are stored as hashes on the server. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable technical measures to protect information.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with infrastructure and service providers that help operate the application, with a calendar provider when you initiate a connection, when required by law or valid legal process, or when necessary to protect rights, safety and service integrity. A salon may share or disclose booking information according to its own privacy practices and legal obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may depend on the salon’s instructions and applicable law. Salon account holders should contact the service operator to request account-level access, correction or deletion. Customers should first contact the salon that collected their information. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. To exercise rights relating to a salon’s customer records, contact that salon. To exercise rights relating to your Salon account or a calendar connection, contact the service operator. You may also revoke Google or Apple access from the provider’s account security settings.

8. Children

The application is designed for salon businesses and their customers, not for children to create independent accounts. A salon is responsible for ensuring that information about minors is collected and used only where permitted and with any required parent or guardian involvement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the current version was published. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to use of the service, subject to applicable law.

10. Contact

For questions about a booking or customer record, contact the salon that manages the booking. For questions about a Salon account, this policy or the application’s handling of account information, contact the service operator through the support channel made available with your Salon service.
